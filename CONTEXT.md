# Wimmelstadt

A living miniature city in the spirit of a Wimmelbilderbuch, where every diecast vehicle its collector has added drives around as a recognisable character among anonymous traffic. It is a place to watch, with no score and no goals.

## Language

### The collection

**Collector**:
The person who owns a collection and the world it lives in. A world has exactly one collector, and today there is only one.
_Avoid_: User, player, account

**Diecast**:
One physical toy vehicle the collector owns or has owned, of any brand, that has wheels and could use a road. Two identical copies are two diecasts.
_Avoid_: Car, model, item, piece

**Casting**:
A manufacturer's tooling of one vehicle shape. Diecasts of the same casting can differ in colour or decals, and each is still its own diecast.
_Avoid_: Model, mould

**Release**:
One thing a maker shipped: a casting in a particular line, year, series and colour.
_Avoid_: Variant, version, edition

**Line**:
The range or rarity class a release belongs to, in collectors' usage: mainline, premium, RLC, Treasure Hunt, chase and so on.
_Avoid_: Tier, category, type

**Twin**:
A diecast that the collector declares alike in every visible detail to another diecast in the collection. Sharing a casting is not enough: another colour or other decals make a different hero.
_Avoid_: Duplicate, copy, double

**Decal**:
A graphic printed on a diecast, such as a number, a stripe or a logo. A hero carries the decals of its diecast.
_Avoid_: Tampo, livery, motif, printed graphics

**Collection**:
All the diecasts the collector has added, including any since parted with.
_Avoid_: Inventory

**Catalogue**:
Every release that a diecast in the collection has been tagged as. It lists what the collector has held, never everything that exists.
_Avoid_: Database, checklist, wish list

**Roster**:
The list of every diecast in the collection, from which the collector can jump straight to a hero, rest it or bring it back.
_Avoid_: Garage, inventory

**Parted**:
Said of a diecast the collector no longer owns. Its hero stays in the world.
_Avoid_: Sold, removed, deleted

**Viewer**:
Somebody the collector has given the PIN to. A viewer sees the heroes in the world and can change nothing.
_Avoid_: Guest, member, user, account

**PIN**:
The one secret the collector hands out to let a viewer see the heroes. Changing it shuts every viewer out until they are given the new one.
_Avoid_: Password, passcode, key

### Making a hero

**Capture**:
The pictures of a diecast from which its hero is made: guided photographs taken by the collector or, for a Mini GT, the maker's own product pictures.
_Avoid_: Scan, shoot, upload

**Finishing**:
The work that makes a hero fit for the world: wheels that spin, lights, true size and faithful decals.
_Avoid_: Post-processing, clean-up, passover

**Bench**:
The place where finishing is done and where the collector approves a hero before it enters the world.
_Avoid_: Testbench, workshop, editor

**Arrival**:
The moment an approved hero first enters the world, brought in by car transporter.
_Avoid_: Spawn, import, unlock

### The world

**World**:
The city in which the heroes and the traffic live, made of tiles. It is permanent: nothing already built ever changes. It grows by whole tiles, when a new place or idea is added, and never as a reward for collecting.
_Avoid_: Map, level, scene

**Tile**:
The piece of ground a district is built on: the size of the look prototype's board, made by hand, filled to its edges, and standing on a base of its own like a model on a table. It needs to know nothing about any other tile.
_Avoid_: Board, chunk, cell, island

**Board**:
Every tile of the world standing edge to edge on one table, each on its own base. A single tile is never called a board.
_Avoid_: Map, grid, overworld

**Plan**:
The record of one tile's layout: its ground, its roads, and where each building and landmark stands. The tile is built from it.
_Avoid_: Layout file, level data, blueprint

**Founding**:
The single act that fixes the world's layout and its name. After it the world is permanent.
_Avoid_: Setup, generation, onboarding

**District**:
A part of the world built around one real or imagined place, with ground, streets and landmarks of its own. It has a tile to itself, or shares one with a neighbour when it is small. No district is another one in different clothes.
_Avoid_: Zone, area, block, chunk, tileset, expansion

**Theme**:
The place a district is built around, which decides its landmarks, its ground, its architecture and the language of its signs. Hong Kong, Delhi, Merseyside, the Alps and Westeros are themes.
_Avoid_: Biome, style, skin

**Quarter**:
A named part of a district that holds more than one place, such as Paris within Europe, Old Delhi within Delhi or the Loop within North America.
_Avoid_: Sub-district, neighbourhood, zone

**Landmark**:
A building or monument whose silhouette is distinctive enough to navigate by. Some are invented, and some are toy versions of famous real ones such as the Sears Tower or the Taj Mahal. A real one stands in the district of its place.
_Avoid_: Monument, icon, wonder

**Cutaway**:
A building with a side or roof left open so that the vehicles inside can be seen.

**Toy size**:
The scale of everything in the world: each vehicle is as large, next to the others, as the physical toy it stands for. A bus the length of a hatchback stays that way.
_Avoid_: Real scale, true scale

**Vehicle**:
Anything that drives in the world: either a hero or part of the traffic.

**Hero**:
A vehicle in the world that stands for exactly one diecast. From its arrival on it is always somewhere in the world, driving or parked where it can be seen, unless it is rested.
_Avoid_: My car, collection car, player car

**Rested**:
Said of a hero the collector has taken out of the world for a while. It keeps its place in the collection and can be brought back at any time.
_Avoid_: Hidden, disabled, garaged, archived

**Traffic**:
The anonymous vehicles that fill the streets and stand for no diecast. They are plain toy cars, so that a hero is told apart by its detail alone.
_Avoid_: NPC, normal car, ambient car

### Life in the world

**Routine**:
A hero's ordinary day: where it drives, parks and idles, and at what hours.
_Avoid_: Schedule, behaviour, AI

**Temperament**:
The character that shapes a hero's routine and how often it shows off. There are four: show-off, cruiser, sleeper and workhorse.
_Avoid_: Personality, class, type

**Antic**:
A showpiece that any hero might perform where it can be seen, such as doughnuts under a bridge or a drift down a spiral ramp.
_Avoid_: Stunt, trick, event

**Party trick**:
A showpiece made by hand for one particular diecast, which no other hero performs.
_Avoid_: Special move, signature move

**Meet**:
A gathering of parked heroes at a set place and hour.

**Vignette**:
A small scene staged in the world for the collector to discover. Tapping one sets off a small reaction.
_Avoid_: Easter egg, event, gag

### Watching

**Explore**:
The camera mode in which the collector moves, turns and zooms the view by hand.

**Drift**:
The camera mode in which the view wanders across the world on its own, leaning towards whatever is happening.

**Follow**:
The camera mode in which the view rides along with one hero.

**Card**:
The small panel that opens when the collector taps a hero, showing what is recorded about its diecast.
_Avoid_: Popup, tooltip, details page
