ADDENDUM: HAUZ KHAS (South Delhi). Fold into the Delhi report as section 8. The first report was already delivered; this adds to it and changes nothing there. Search coverage was thin (heritage-walk blogs and travel sites), so layout and businesses are mostly background knowledge and should be spot-checked. Verified by search: Firoz Shah Tughlaq (reigned 1351 to 1388) restored the old tank, the complex holds his madrasa, a mosque and his tomb, Deer Park has deer and rabbit enclosures, Hauz Khas Metro is on the Yellow Line, and Green Park and Aurobindo Market lie on the walking route to the village.

Layout in plain terms: the lake is a long rectangle. The tomb and madrasa ruins form an L along its north and east edge. Hauz Khas Village lanes press against the east/south side. Deer Park lies north/northwest. Green Park and Aurobindo Market are south, IIT Delhi and Siri Fort are further west and east.

- Hauz Khas Lake / royal tank: a long, rectangular reservoir with stone steps, edged with trees and often green-brown. Sunken lawn-ringed pond. The anchor: everything else is placed around it.
- Firoz Shah Tughlaq's tomb: squat, square, thick-walled grey-brown block with a flat, shallow dome and a door. On the lake's southeast corner.
- Madrasa ruins: long L of grey rubble-stone walls, two storeys of arches, with chhatri-capped corner pavilions and domed roof bumps. Along the lake's north-east edge, overlooking the water.
- Deer Park: big green wedge with trees, paths and small tombs, deer enclosure in a corner. North and northwest of the lake.
- Hauz Khas Village: narrow lanes of 3 to 4 storey whitewashed or exposed-brick buildings with rooftop cafes, balconies, awnings, fairy lights, boutiques, galleries and hand-painted or neon signs in English. Squeezed against the lake's southeast side. Pedestrian-ish; no road through any monument.
- Green Park: tidy residential grid and a small market, white and cream low-rises, south of the village.
- Aurobindo Market: strip of shops and food stalls along Aurobindo Marg, a straight avenue just east of the area.
- Hauz Khas Metro: elevated station with a glass-and-steel roof, on the Yellow Line. Magenta Line is also nearby but I did not confirm which station is closest to the complex.
- Siri Fort: round, low-walled arena (the Siri Fort Auditorium and Sports Complex) plus a ruined fort wall, east of Aurobindo Marg.
- IIT Delhi: a campus of long, plain brick-and-concrete blocks with small greens, to the west of the lake.

Real operating places (verify before using on a sign): Hauz Khas Social (cafe and bar in the village); Kunzum Travel Cafe (small cafe and bookshop); Gulati and Hauz Khas Market stores; Deer Park (public park); IIT Delhi (institute); Siri Fort Auditorium (arts venue); Aurobindo Market shops (local grocers and tailors). I did not confirm each is currently open.

Three small scenes: (1) a morning jogging and yoga group in Deer Park among peacocks and deer; (2) rooftop cafe evening with fairy lights and a small band over the village lanes, overlooking the lake; (3) ducks and a duckling parade on the lake steps beside the madrasa ruins, with a photographer.

Sensitivity: Firoz Shah's tomb is a tomb and the site is a protected heritage monument, so no cars, no gags, and no climbing figures on it. A note to the toy city: the complex has been under conservation in places, so check current access before relying on it.
