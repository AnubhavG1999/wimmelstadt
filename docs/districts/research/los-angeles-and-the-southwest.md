## A. Los Angeles

**1. Character.** A flat, pale grid of low roofs and palm-lined boulevards fills a basin, with one small clump of glass towers. Scrubby hills along the north carry the white sign and a three-domed observatory. Freeways knot into stacked interchanges, and the river is a concrete trough. Beach, pier funfair and a harbour liner close the west and south.

**2. Ground.**
- Basin: flat plain open to the ocean west and south (Long Beach harbour).
- Hills: the Santa Monica Mountains line the north edge. Sign and observatory sit at their east end, facing south; Mulholland Drive follows the crest west. Behind lies the flat Valley (Burbank), then the higher San Gabriels (Angeles Crest).
- River: from the Valley round the hills' east end, past downtown's east side, then south to Long Beach beside the Queen Mary.
- Downtown: inland at that bend; Dodger Stadium on the hill just north.
- Wilshire Boulevard runs about 16 miles west from downtown to the ocean by the pier. South along the shore: Venice (letter sign over the street, canals with arched footbridges), LAX, Long Beach.
- Coast north, true order: Malibu, Bixby Bridge, then Laguna Seca (inland) and Pebble Beach.

**3. Landmarks.**
1. Hollywood Sign: nine white 45 ft letters on Mount Lee, mast behind. No road.
2. Griffith Observatory: white Art Deco block, three copper domes, on a spur south-east of the sign; a road loops up.
3. Santa Monica Pier: timber deck with Ferris wheel, yellow coaster, carousel shed. Cars drive under a blue arched sign and down a ramp. The symbolic "End of the Trail" Route 66 sign is on deck.
4. Downtown: City Hall (white stepped tower, pyramid cap), Disney Hall (crumpled steel sails), Wilshire Grand (tallest; glass sail with spire), U.S. Bank Tower (round crown).
5. Sixth Street Viaduct: ten pairs of leaning concrete arches; road over river, rails and freeway; Whittier Boulevard continues east.
6. Capitol Records: 13-storey stack-of-discs cylinder with spire, just off Hollywood Boulevard (pavement stars).
7. Randy's Donuts: tiny drive-in under a 32 ft brown donut, beside the 405 north-east of LAX.
8. LAX Theme Building: white saucer under crossed arches; the terminal loop road circles it.
9. Queen Mary: black hull, white decks, three red funnels; opposite the Grand Prix circuit across the harbour.
10. Dodger Stadium: pastel tiers, zigzag roofs, rings of terraced car parks.

**4. Roads and car culture.**
- Coast road southbound: Pebble Beach (concours on the 18th fairway; 75th held 16 Aug 2026); Laguna Seca (2.238 miles, 11 turns; the blind downhill left-right, renamed "Zanardi Corkscrew" on 4 Sep 2026); Bixby Bridge (1932 concrete open arch, road over); Big Sur (fully open since 14 Jan 2026); Malibu; pier.
- Mulholland Drive: two-lane ridge road, overlooks both ways. Angeles Crest Highway: 66 miles, up to 7,903 ft.
- Four Level Interchange (first stack; 101/110, downtown's north edge). Pregerson Interchange (105/110; taller, carpool flyovers, rail station inside; east of LAX).
- Petersen museum: red box wrapped in steel ribbons, Wilshire at Fairfax. Just east, LACMA's new concrete galleries (May 2026) bridge Wilshire.
- Bob's Big Boy, Burbank: car show Fridays 4–10 pm, carhops at weekends.
- Whittier Boulevard, East LA: lowriders under the boulevard arch; cruising bans void since 1 Jan 2024.
- Long Beach Grand Prix: 1.968 miles, 11 turns (Shoreline Drive, fountain, hairpin); April.

**5. Businesses.** Bob's Big Boy (pink-and-white neon sign, statue); Original Pantry Cafe (reopens 19 Nov 2026). Not re-checked (u): Randy's Donuts, Pink's Hot Dogs (pink stand), Tail o' the Pup (hot-dog-shaped stand), The Donut Hole (drive through two giant donuts). Parodies: "La Brea Tar & Lube", "Mulholland Drive-Thru", "Rodeo Drive-In".

**6. Street life.** Black-and-white police SUVs; orange city buses (u); white driverless Jaguars (u); few yellow cabs (u); candy-paint Impalas on wire wheels, one corner lifted; taco trucks; fruit carts with rainbow umbrellas; pale blue lifeguard towers and yellow lifeguard pickups (u); skinny fan palms, blue street signs, nodding oil pumps.

**7. Small scenes.** Friday cruise night in the Big Boy car park. Lowriders hopping under the Whittier arch, families in lawn chairs. Film crew staging a chase in the river bed. Freeway-ramp jam with drivers dancing on car roofs. Prewar cars rolling onto the Pebble Beach fairway at dawn.

**8. Watch out.**
- January 2025 fires: Palisades (12 dead) destroyed Pacific Palisades and Malibu's coast-road strip (Will Rogers ranch house, Reel Inn, Moonshadows); Eaton (at least 18 dead) much of Altadena. Sign and observatory unharmed (u).
- Angeles Crest: about 30 miles shut "indefinitely" after Christmas 2025 storms; current status (u).
- Viaduct lights dark after copper theft (relight due by 2028); park beneath due early 2027.
- LAX people mover not before January 2027; Theme Building restaurant shut 2013, deck 2018.
- Cinerama Dome shut since 2020, reopening planned; Irwindale Speedway closed December 2024 (u).
- Rights: the Hollywood Chamber of Commerce claims trademark in the sign and seeks fees for commercial depictions. Avoid team and chain logos.
- Sensitivities: fire losses; the Chavez Ravine evictions beneath Dodger Stadium; lowriding is Chicano heritage, not gang shorthand.

## B. Las Vegas and the desert Southwest

**1. Character.** One dense line of neon toys (pyramid, glowing ball, needle tower, fountain lake) stands alone on a tan desert floor ringed by bare mountains. Elsewhere the ground is the landmark: white salt, red buttes, a stepped canyon, a dam in a gorge. One two-lane road of roadside oddities crosses it all.

**2. Ground.**
- Spine: Route 66 westward over prairie (Oklahoma, Texas), red mesas (New Mexico), painted badlands and pine plateau (Arizona), then hairpins down to the Colorado.
- North of it, east to west: Monument Valley; Grand Canyon (spur road north to the South Rim); Las Vegas (from Kingman by US 93).
- River: the Colorado leaves the canyon for Lake Mead, passes Hoover Dam (30 miles south-east of the Strip), turns south, crosses Route 66 and becomes the Los Angeles edge.
- The Strip, south to north along one boulevard: Welcome sign, Luxor, Bellagio, Sphere (a block east), Strat, Fremont Street, Neon Museum.
- Bonneville really lies about 400 miles north, in Utah.
- Ghost town: Nelson, in a canyon between Las Vegas and the river (rusting cars, old pumps).

**3. Landmarks.**
1. Sphere (official name just "Sphere" since 2023): LED ball 366 ft high, 516 ft wide; the race track loops round it.
2. Welcome sign: 25 ft stretched white diamond on two poles, red star, seven discs spelling WELCOME; in the boulevard median.
3. Luxor: 350 ft black glass pyramid, sphinx, white sky beam.
4. Bellagio: curved cream hotel behind a lake on the Strip; fountain jets.
5. The Strat: 1,149 ft concrete stalk with saucer pod and rides.
6. Fremont Street: vaulted LED canopy over a pedestrian street, neon cowboy; cross streets pass under.
7. Neon Museum: yard of retired signs, white shell-shaped lobby.
8. Hoover Dam: pale curved concrete wedge, 726 ft, four intake towers, road on the crest; an arch bridge downstream carries US 93.
9. Monument Valley: two "mitten" buttes and Merrick Butte, red, about 1,000 ft; US 163 runs straight at them.
10. Grand Canyon: banded red steps, thin river, round stone watchtower on the South Rim.

**4. Roads and car culture.**
- Route 66 westbound in true road order, open unless noted: U-Drop Inn, Shamrock (1936 Art Deco station, two towers, pink and green; visitor centre; café (u)); Cadillac Ranch (ten finned Cadillacs nose-down in a row, graffiti; free); Blue Swallow Motel (pink stucco, neon swallow); Petrified Forest (rusty 1932 Studebaker marks the old road) (u); Wigwam Motel (15 white concrete teepees, old cars outside); Meteor Crater (u); Seligman (Snow Cap drive-in (u); Angel Delgadillo, 99; family's 1923 barbershop restored April 2026); Hackberry General Store (pumps, red Corvette); hairpins into Oatman (wild burros, staged gunfights); then, west of the river, Roy's in Amboy (sign, petrol and souvenirs only; café and cabins still shut).
- Centenary: birthday 11 Nov 2026; new and restored neon (Tulsa above all), visitor centres, bridges. No National Historic Trail status yet.
- Formula 1 circuit: 6.201 km, 17 turns, anticlockwise, night; pits at Harmon and Koval, round Sphere, 1.9 km down the Strip past Bellagio. Race 21 Nov 2026; contracted to 2037.
- Hoover Dam crest: former US 93; visitor cars enter by a Nevada checkpoint and dead-end on the Arizona side.
- Bonneville: 12 by 5 mile salt pan; the access road just ends. Speed Week ran in August 2026 (78th; about 240 entries; crust reported ¼ inch thin).

**5. Businesses.** Jack Rabbit Trading Post (yellow "HERE IT IS" billboard). Not re-checked (u): Golden Nugget (white and gold), Flamingo (pink neon plumes), Peppermill (purple neon diner), A Little White Wedding Chapel (drive-through), Pinball Hall of Fame (giant red letters), Big Texan Steak Ranch (yellow barn, giant steer). Parodies: "The Golden Gasket Casino", "U-Tow Inn", "Lost Wages Motor Lodge".

**6. Street life.** Double-decker Deuce buses, all night; taxis in several company colours (u); black-and-white police (u); stretch limos, billboard trucks, pink wedding Cadillacs, boxy Zoox robotaxis (u); escalator footbridges over Strip junctions; race fencing and light rigs each November. Desert: long freight trains beside the road (u), route shields painted on asphalt, rhyming Burma-Shave signs, Joshua trees. No saguaros here.

**7. Small scenes.** Visitors spray-painting the Cadillacs. A burro's head in a car window, traffic stuck behind. Elvis marrying a couple at a drive-through window. A streamliner and push truck alone on white salt. Marshals and a safety car under the glowing Sphere.

**8. Watch out.**
- Gone or changed: Tropicana imploded 9 Oct 2024 (ballpark due 2028); Mirage shut 17 Jul 2024, becoming a guitar-shaped Hard Rock (2027); the Neon Museum's planned move was dropped in 2025; Luxor demolition is rumour only.
- Grand Canyon Lodge (North Rim) burnt July 2025. Hoover Dam shut 27–31 Jul 2026, now open. Old road east of Amboy closed (April 2026).
- Rights: the Welcome sign's designer meant it for the public domain. Treat Sphere imagery, casino names, Formula 1 marks and Cadillac Ranch (artists' copyright) as protected (u).
- Sensitivities: Monument Valley is Navajo land (drones banned); teepee motels and trading-post kitsch are stereotypes; the 2017 shooting site faces Luxor (u); a driver died at Speed Week 2025 (u).
