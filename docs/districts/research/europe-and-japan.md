# DISTRICT A: EUROPE

## LONDON (Liverpool excluded)
**Landmarks (silhouettes)**
- Tower Bridge: two Gothic stone towers with a blue-painted high walkway and a pair of bascule leaves in the middle; the leaves can be animated to lift.
- St Paul's Cathedral: a big grey-white dome on a drum, a lantern on top, and two small west towers.
- London Eye: a tall thin white wheel on an A-frame leg, with oval pods.
- Tower of London: a squat square White Tower with four corner turrets, inside low walls and round towers.
- Optional: the Gherkin, a bullet-shaped glass tower.

**Geography**
- The Thames is a wide brown S-curve that cuts the district. Tower Bridge crosses it at the east end, next to the Tower of London on the north bank.
- Big Ben and the Houses of Parliament sit on the north bank at a bend.
- The London Eye is on the opposite south bank.
- St Paul's is inland on the north bank, on Ludgate Hill, a small rise.
- Bridges between Westminster and Tower Bridge are plain grey or green spans, with Westminster Bridge green.
- Tower Bridge opens (roughly 800 lifts a year) when a ship needs to pass, so it makes a good timed event.

**Ordinary streets**
- Terraced brick houses in brown and yellow stock brick, with grey slate pitched roofs and many chimney pots.
- White stucco rows near Westminster.
- Narrow streets, red pillar boxes, and some green squares with plane trees.

**Businesses**
- Harrods
- Fortnum & Mason (eau-de-nil/duck-egg blue and gold)
- Hamleys
- Twinings (Strand)
- Pret a Manger
- Boots
- Borough Market is the best-known market.

**Vehicles**
- Black cabs (black, orange light)
- Red double-decker buses (red with a white/grey roof stripe)
- Blue police cars with a yellow checker
- Royal Mail red vans
- Dark-green/black Brompton-style bikes

**Scenes**
1. Tower Bridge lifts, a tall ship passes, and traffic waits with the buses.
2. A Beefeater and a ravens' lawn at the Tower, with tourists.

## PARIS
**Landmarks**
- Arc de Triomphe: a big flat-topped stone arch, with a round junction around it (Place Charles de Gaulle, "l'Étoile") and 12 avenues spoking out.
- Notre-Dame: twin square towers and a thin central spire, with flying buttresses on the Île de la Cité (reopened Dec 2024).
- Sacré-Cœur: white domes on top of Montmartre hill.
- Louvre pyramid: a glass pyramid in a stone U-shaped palace courtyard.
- Moulin Rouge: a red windmill on a low block.

**Geography**
- The Seine curves through the centre with the Île de la Cité island in it.
- Many stone bridges cross it; Pont Neuf, Pont Alexandre III (gilded lamps) and Pont des Arts are the best known.
- Bridges are low, arched and cream-stone.
- The Eiffel Tower is on the south bank at a bend, on the Champ de Mars lawn.
- The Arc is on a rise to the west at the star junction; the Champs-Élysées leads to it.
- Montmartre is a hill to the north, with stepped streets, vineyards and Sacré-Cœur on top.

**Ordinary streets**
- Haussmann blocks: cream-limestone, 6 storeys, wrought-iron balconies on floors 2 and 5, grey zinc mansard roofs with chimney pots.
- Wedge-shaped corners at the avenue crossings.
- Café terraces with red/green awnings and round tables, plane trees along the boulevards.

**Businesses**
- Ladurée (pastel green boxes)
- Angelina (Rue de Rivoli)
- Café de Flore (green awning and rattan chairs)
- Galeries Lafayette (glass cupola)
- Printemps
- Poilâne bakery

**Vehicles**
- Dark/black Peugeot and Renault taxis with a roof light
- Blue-and-cream RATP buses
- Velib bikes (green/silver)
- Citroën 2CV and Renault 4 for charm
- White Paris Metro entrances in green Art Nouveau (Guimard)

**Scenes**
1. A baguette cyclist and a café terrace under the Eiffel Tower.
2. Painters at easels on Montmartre's Place du Tertre.

## ITALY
**Landmarks**
- Colosseum: an oval, open arched ring in travertine.
- St Peter's Basilica (Vatican): a huge ribbed dome on a squared body with a colonnade in an oval ring.
- Florence Duomo: a red terracotta dome, with a pink/white/green marble body and a Giotto campanile.
- Venice's Rialto Bridge: a single stone arch with shops on it, over a canal.
- Pisa pieces: the Duomo and Baptistery (round, domed) next to the Leaning Tower.

**Geography**
- A real Italy district needs a spine of hills and a coast.
- Pisa's Piazza dei Miracoli (Field of Miracles) is a flat green lawn with the Cathedral, Baptistery, Leaning Tower and Camposanto in a row, ringed by a white wall.
- The Arno runs through Pisa and Florence with stone bridges (Ponte Vecchio, covered, with shops over it).
- Rome is on seven hills and the Tiber.
- Venice is a canal grid.
- Possible split: Pisa/Florence on the Arno plain, Rome beyond, Venice at the edge on the water.

**Ordinary streets**
- Ochre, orange, pink and cream plaster walls with green or brown shutters.
- Low terracotta pantile roofs, narrow lanes and arches.
- Piazzas with a central fountain and a church, with cafe tables.
- Cypress and umbrella pines on the hills.

**Businesses**
- Lavazza
- illy
- Ferrari (Maranello)
- Vespa (made by Piaggio in Pontedera, near Pisa)
- Eataly
- Gucci
- Barilla
- Campari

**Vehicles**
- Vespa and Lambretta scooters in pastel
- Fiat 500 (cream, light blue, red)
- Ape three-wheel vans (blue/white)
- White Italian taxis
- Carabinieri (dark blue with a red stripe) and Polizia (pale blue and white)
- Vaporetto water buses in Venice

**Scenes**
1. Scooters circle a fountain piazza at the aperitivo hour.
2. A tourist pretending to hold up the Leaning Tower on the lawn.

# DISTRICT B: JAPAN

## Tokyo and Kyoto icons
- **Tokyo Tower**: red-and-white lattice Eiffel-like tower with two decks; stands on a low block in Minato, near Zojoji temple.
- **Tokyo Skytree**: a tall pale-silver tapering tripod becoming round, with two bulbs on the shaft; sits at the far east edge by the Sumida River, next to a wide mall block.
- **Shibuya Crossing**: an X-shaped scramble crossing with big LED screens; sits in the centre, in front of Hachiko's statue and Shibuya Station.
- **Kaminarimon, Senso-ji**: red gate with a huge red paper lantern with black lettering and two guardian statues; leads to the Nakamise shop lane, then a hall with a five-storey pagoda behind it.
- **Fushimi Inari**: a hill with a path of hundreds of red torii gates; a forested cone with a path up one side.
- **Mount Fuji**: a snow-capped cone beyond water, as a backdrop. In reality it is about 100 km from Tokyo; at model scale, put it behind the water.
- **Shinkansen**: a long white train with a blue stripe (Tokaido line) and a long pointed nose; run it on an elevated viaduct.
- **Others**: Tokyo Station's red-brick building, Meiji Jingu's giant wooden torii, Kinkaku-ji (gold pavilion with a phoenix), Himeji/Osaka-style castle keep with white walls and tiered roofs, Rainbow Bridge.

**Ordinary streets from above**
- Dense, small buildings of 2-5 floors.
- Grey/blue tile roofs on old houses and flat concrete roofs with water tanks.
- Overhead power lines and cables.
- Narrow lanes, vending machines, a pocket park.
- Kyoto has low wooden machiya with dark tile roofs; Tokyo has tall thin pencil buildings.
- Rooftop signs everywhere.

**Businesses**
1. 7-Eleven: orange, green and red stripes on a white box.
2. FamilyMart: green and blue stripes.
3. Lawson: a blue sign with a milk bottle.
4. Ichiran ramen: a red-and-white sign.
5. Don Quijote ("Donki"): a bright yellow/red façade with a penguin mascot (Donpen), stacked goods.
6. Yoshinoya: orange beef bowls.
7. Sukiya: orange sign with Japanese letters.
8. Matsuya: a yellow sign.
9. Uniqlo: red square logo.
10. Daiso: a red sign.
11. Yodobashi Camera: a huge sign and a jingle (optional).

**Vehicles**
- Taxis in Tokyo are mostly Toyota Crown or JPN Taxi, in black, yellow or green.
- Kei cars (small white cars with a yellow plate).
- Mini trucks (kei trucks).
- Buses: Toei in green/white, Kyoto city buses in cream with a green stripe.
- Trains: JR Yamanote in light green, Keikyu in red and white, Tokyo Metro Ginza line in orange.
- Bicycles (mamachari) everywhere.

**Signs and scripts**
- Vertical neon signs in katakana and kanji (ラーメン, 居酒屋, コンビニ, 薬 for pharmacy, 営業中 for open).
- Red paper lanterns (chochin) at izakaya.
- Yellow and blue street signs; torii in vermilion with black caps.
- Keep the text short on the model: kana and a few kanji read as a texture.

**Scenes**
1. Shibuya scramble: dots of pedestrians cross in all directions as the light turns.
2. The big lantern at Kaminarimon, with rickshaw runners.
3. A Shinkansen passes with Mount Fuji behind it.
4. Torii tunnel with a fox (kitsune) statue and a small shrine at the top.

# RECENT CHANGES (found via search)
- **Tokyo Skytree**: temporarily closed after an elevator incident with 20 visitors trapped; reopened Feb 26, 2026.
- **Shibuya**: the Tsutaya building (and the Starbucks with a crossing view) is closed for renovation; the crossing itself remains open.
- **Eiffel Tower**: summit closed Jan 5 to Feb 6, 2026 for maintenance; closed early for heatwaves; closed for a staff walkout around Sept 7, 2026. Demolition rumours are false (Fodor's).
- **Tower Bridge**: overnight and short closures for maintenance (Jan and Apr 2026).
- **Albert Bridge, London**: closed for repairs since Feb 2026, reportedly into 2027.
- **Miraikan (Tokyo)**: closed Oct 1, 2026 to Apr 22, 2027.
- **Seven & i**: Ito-Yokado cutting stores; 7-Eleven (North America) closing about 645 stores in 2026. The brand stays in Japan.
- **Not found**: no closure, rename or demolition reports for Tokyo Tower, Senso-ji or Kaminarimon, Fushimi Inari, Big Ben, or the Harrods, Fortnum & Mason, Hamleys, Ichiran and Donki brands.
