DISTRICT B: AMUSEMENT PARK

Parks and signatures
- Coney Island, Brooklyn: Cyclone (1927 white-framed wooden coaster, red cars, tight drops); Deno's Wonder Wheel (1920, 150 ft, eccentric wheel with swinging and fixed cars, spokes and arcs); Parachute Jump (1939 red lattice tower, skeleton only, no chutes); Luna Park (Thunderbolt coaster, Astro Tower, midway); Nathan's at Surf & Stillwell; boardwalk and beach. [K]. 2026: Luna Park and Wonder Wheel opened Mar 28-29; the Cyclone began its 99th season [V]; lease renewed through 2027 [V]; Astro Tower is reported returned in a new format and a steel Thunderbolt is being built (completion date unconfirmed) [V].
- Santa Monica Pier (Pacific Park): Pacific Wheel (solar LED Ferris wheel, 130 ft, 40 spokes, over the ocean); West Coaster (compact steel coaster); Sea Dragon swinging ship; wood pier with arched "Santa Monica" sign and carousel (Looff Hippodrome). Pacific Park marked 30 years in May 2026; 12 rides; free entry [V].
- Blackpool Pleasure Beach: The Big One (1994 tall steel coaster, 213 ft, dropped out of lift, blue-and-white); Big Dipper (1923 wooden coaster; stopped July 23, 2026 during an operating stop, and reportedly remains closed in one report, status uncertain) [V]; Icon (2018 launch coaster) ; Valhalla (dark indoor water ride, Viking exterior) ; Grand National (wooden racing coaster) ; Avalanche; Aviktas (new in 2026, a 138 ft gyroswing, said to be the UK's tallest) [V]; near Blackpool Tower. 
- Brighton Palace Pier: 1899 Victorian iron pier on piles, domed pavilions and a clock, with a funfair at the seaward end. Rides: Crazy Mouse (spinning wild mouse, since 2000), Turbo Coaster, Air Race, Wild River, Galaxia; Booster was removed (2022-23) [V]. The pier was put up for sale Jan 2026 via Knight Frank; no buyer reported in results [V].
- Tivoli Gardens, Copenhagen: not on the sea (it is by the city centre) but is the classic inspiration; Rutschebanen (1914 wooden coaster in a mountain-like structure, brakeman rides each train, still running) ; Star Flyer (80 m swing tower); Pantomime Theatre (Chinese-style peacock-tail curtain, open air); onion-domed Moorish pavilion, lake with lights. 2026 season 27 Mar to 20 Sep plus Halloween and Christmas [V].
- Others that matter: Cedar Point, Ohio (coaster capital, on Lake Erie) [K]; Belmont Park, San Diego (1925 Giant Dipper wooden coaster on the beach) [K]; Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk (1924 Giant Dipper and Looff carousel) [K]; Morey's Piers and Seaside Heights, New Jersey [K]; Kennywood (not coastal) [K]; Southend Adventure Island [K]; Dreamland Margate (1920 Scenic Railway, coaster) [K]; Gröna Lund, Stockholm (island, with water on its edge) [K]; Pleasure Pier, Galveston [K].

How they sit on the water
- Piers: a long timber or iron deck on piles, straight out from the promenade, with rides at the far end (Brighton, Santa Monica); water visible under gaps; a big wheel at the end of the pier.
- Boardwalk and beach: parks sit behind a boardwalk, with the sea beyond (Coney Island); wide beach, groynes.
- Island shoreline for the model: boardwalk along the shore, a pier jutting into the sea, a beach strip at the edge to blend with neighbours.

What reads best from above
- Wheel (round, big, the most legible shape from a 38 degree view); coaster track (sinuous lines and a lift hill; the white wooden frame silhouette as at the Cyclone); swing tower and star flyer (a tall mast with a circle of seats); drop tower; carousel (round with a conical roof); pirate-ship and swinging ship (crescent); Ferris wheels with lit spokes; midway stalls in small colourful clusters and flags; a lit sign and entrance arch.
