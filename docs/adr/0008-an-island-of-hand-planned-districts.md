# An island of hand-planned districts

**Discarded on 19 November 2026 and replaced by [0009](0009-a-city-of-hand-built-tiles.md).** The island was built as far as its whole ground, twenty-three landmarks in their real shape and traffic on its roads. Set beside the look prototype, its density and its layout did not hold up: it was too large to build by hand, and the ordinary buildings that filled the ground between its landmarks were made by rule. It is kept on the branch `island-prototype`. What this record says about names, about care for places of worship, tombs and memorials, and about Westeros still stands.

The world is an island with smaller islands round it, and every district on it is planned by hand around one real or imagined place: Hong Kong, Delhi, Merseyside, the Alps, Westeros. A district's landmarks stand where that place's own geography would put them, its ground and its street pattern are its own, it adds one road worth driving, and it blends into its neighbours across a seam instead of meeting them at a line. The plan is kept as data in `app/src/island`, and both the map in `docs/island.svg` and the city itself are made from it. The look prototype's themed blocks were one layout in different clothes. On seeing them the collector asked for districts that keep their own identity and are never a reskin of one another, for varied ground, and for water on every side so that the city no longer ends at the edge of a tile, and then for many more districts: twenty-three in all. We gave up a city laid out by a generator, which [0002](0002-the-world-only-grows.md) assumed and the brief had left open, and with it a layout that could be rolled again before the founding. A generator makes endless city of one kind. A Wimmelbuch page is drawn.

## Consequences

- The founding is now the moment the plan is agreed. Until then the plan may be redrawn freely, and it has already grown from nine districts to twenty-three. After it, 0002 holds: what the plan fixes never changes, which is the coast, the roads and where each landmark stands. A district added later takes sea, as a new island or new coast, and leaves the rest alone.
- The land covers about 1.36 million square units, many times the prototype's one board. [0006](0006-the-whole-city-from-the-first-day.md) says the city's size is set by what the Duo can draw and hold, and that still stands. So the city has to be drawn in levels of detail, with only the districts near the camera in full. Whether the Duo can hold it has not been shown, and it is the largest risk in the plan.
- The island is whole from the start: coast, water, high ground and main roads come first, for all of it. Each district's detail follows, one district at a time.
- Real places bring real names. Every district has research notes in `docs/districts/research`. A business's name is checked before it goes on a sign, and stays without a logo. Places of worship, tombs and memorials are drawn with care and are never the setting for a joke. Nothing political is shown.
- Westeros is the one imagined place so far. It is a homage in the project's own drawing, after the books' descriptions: no logos, no title lettering and no likenesses.
- Every island can be reached on wheels, by bridge, tunnel or car ferry.
- There is still no toy track and there are no loops. A race circuit is a road.
