# No imported catalogue

Wimmelstadt holds no list of everything Hot Wheels or Mini GT have made. Its catalogue is only what the collector has tagged: a Hot Wheels diecast can be linked to its casting's page on the Hot Wheels fan wiki, and a Mini GT diecast is identified by its MGT number. We looked for a source to import and found none worth it: neither maker publishes one, the one commercial database is offered by arrangement for a four-figure setup fee, and the fan wiki licenses its text for reuse with attribution but not its images.

## Consequences

- Outside Wimmelstadt, a casting's identity is its fan wiki page or its MGT number.
- A lookup only proposes. The tags the collector confirms are the record, and a later lookup never overwrites them.
- Wimmelstadt stores the link and never a copy of the wiki's images, and shows attribution wherever wiki text appears.
- "Which releases am I missing?" cannot be answered. That is deliberate: Wimmelstadt is not a collection manager.
