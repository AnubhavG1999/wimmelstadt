# A Mini GT can be made from Mini GT's own pictures

A Mini GT diecast can be captured from the three product pictures Mini GT publishes for each car, instead of being photographed by the collector. The first generator trial, on 18 November 2026, made the black Countach LB-WORKS (MGT01081) from those three pictures alone, and the collector judged it good enough, "especially since the cars will be fairly small". It spares most of the collection an evening in the diorama set and lets many more cars be added. It qualifies [0003](0003-heroes-are-generated-from-photos.md): a hero is still generated and finished as that record says, but the capture of a Mini GT may be the maker's pictures rather than the collector's photographs.

## Considered Options

- **Photographing every diecast, as first planned.** Larger, sharper pictures of the collector's own copy from six angles, but slow work for a backlog of 30 to 50 cars.
- **Mini GT's pictures topped up with a few of the collector's own shots** for the views they miss: the top, the right side and the back. Kept for any car where those views matter.

## Consequences

- Mini GT's pictures show a prototype sample of one version, usually the left-hand-drive one. Where the collector's copy differs, it is photographed.
- The pictures are small, 900 by 600 on older items, so fine print does not survive. At the size a car is seen in the city this does not matter, and the decals that do, the numbers and the big names, came through.
- The pictures belong to Mini GT. They stay in the private captures folder and are never published.
- Hot Wheels publishes no such pictures, so its diecasts are still photographed.
- These pictures would let in cars the collector does not own. Whether such cars may become heroes is not decided.
