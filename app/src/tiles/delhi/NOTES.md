# Delhi: notes

What a later reader cannot see in the code. The tile is not built yet: this is the layout as it was put to the collector on 19 November 2026, before any building. Whatever changes once he has answered is changed here.

The research is in `docs/districts/research/`: `delhi-for-the-tile.md`, and the two notes it builds on, `delhi.md` and `delhi-hauz-khas.md`.

## How the tile lies

North is at the top of the board, as on a map. Nothing is turned except Hauz Khas (see the liberties).

- **The Yamuna** runs down the east side from the north edge to the south edge, 18 wide, with ghats on its west bank and a strip of east bank beyond it, 16 wide. The water is brown-green, not blue.
- **Old Delhi** is the north: Chandni Chowk from the Red Fort's Lahori Gate west to Fatehpuri Masjid, Jama Masjid south of it, the Red Fort between Netaji Subhash Marg and the river.
- **Connaught Place** is west of the middle, with Karol Bagh west of it.
- **The axis** runs east and west across the middle: Rashtrapati Bhavan on Raisina Hill at the west edge, the two Blocks, Vijay Chowk, Kartavya Path between its lawns and canals, and India Gate in its hexagon.
- **South of the axis and inside the Ring Road**: Sarojini Nagar, Safdarjung's Tomb, Dilli Haat, Lodhi Garden, Khan Market, the National Stadium, Humayun's Tomb.
- **South of the Ring Road**: R. K. Puram and the school in the west, Hauz Khas and the Qutub Minar in the middle, the Lotus Temple in the east.
- **The east bank**: the Signature Bridge's landing in the north, Akshardham in the middle, the Metro's station in the south.
- **Raisina Hill** is the tile's one piece of relief, a terrace 3 high. The Ridge is woods south of it.

## Roads

Traffic keeps left. No road crosses another on the level, and no lane is shared.

- **The Ring Road** is one road there and back, with a turning loop at each end: Dhaula Kuan's loop in the west, east along the south over a flyover at Moti Bagh and another at AIIMS, north beside the river behind the Red Fort, over the Signature Bridge, and down the east bank to Akshardham's gate. A lane each way, with a verge between them on the south leg where the Metro's piers stand.
- **The long drive** is one road there and back: from the Qutub Minar north up Aurobindo Marg under the AIIMS flyover, past Safdarjung's Tomb to the hexagon, a third of the way round it, west along Kartavya Path and up Raisina Hill to turn in front of Rashtrapati Bhavan's gates, back along Kartavya Path, the long way round India Gate, and back down to the Qutub. It uses the hexagon's outer lane exactly once round. The hexagon's inner lane is a loop of its own.
- **Shanti Path** runs from below South Block under the Moti Bagh flyover to R. K. Puram and along Kaifi Azmi Marg to the school's gate, where the buses turn.
- **Connaught Place**: the Inner Circle is a loop. The Outer Circle's inner lane is a loop. Its outer lane belongs to one road there and back that runs from Karol Bagh to Connaught Place, round part of it, east and north up Netaji Subhash Marg past Jama Masjid and the Red Fort to Kashmere Gate, and back round the rest of it.
- **Chandni Chowk and the lanes of Old Delhi** carry no motor traffic: cycle rickshaws and autos go on lines of their own.
- **The Metro** runs on its viaduct along the Ring Road's south leg, over both flyovers, and on across the Yamuna to a station on the east bank.

## Places for heroes

Five on show, each open to the south-east: the turntable in the Auto Expo's hall at Bharat Mandapam, the line of the vintage car rally at the National Stadium, the fitting bay on Karol Bagh's accessory street, a bay in Khan Market's car park, and a bay on Connaught Place's Inner Circle. None is at a place of worship, a tomb or a memorial.

On the roads a hero is best seen on Kartavya Path, on the flyovers, beside the river, on the Signature Bridge, on the east bank, and on Kaifi Azmi Marg in front of the school.

## The screen for music

One, on the roof of Connaught Place's south-east block, where its hoardings stand in life. It faces the south-east, and it is not near a place of worship, a tomb or a memorial. It is hung as an ordinary sign of kind `screen`, which the engine fills with what is playing. North America's gate tower has one: see `SIGNS` in its `plan.ts`. Nothing for the music is built in this folder.

A second screen was planned on Bharat Mandapam's hall and is dropped: one is enough for a tile. The hall gets a painted board.

**Its own picture.** When nothing is playing, and for everyone who has not linked their music, the screen shows a picture the tile paints itself, so that picture is what most people see. The sign gets a `paint(ctx, s, w, h)` of its own, into 640 by 360 pixels. It draws the whole rectangle each time and keeps nothing between calls.

Planned: the tailboard of a lorry. HORN OK PLEASE in the colours of truck art, in three panels, with lotus flowers and a painted pair of eyes, and under it फिर मिलेंगे, "we'll meet again", which lorries carry too. It is bold enough to read from across the tile, it belongs on a tile about cars, and it does not look like a display of what is playing. No brand and no logo is on it. The Hindi is checked in its own script before it is painted, like every other sign.

The other idea, if the collector prefers it: दिल्ली दिलवालों की, "Delhi belongs to the big-hearted", round a heart.

## Sight lines

Asked of the layout with `hiddenBy`'s sums, for 67 points: every place for a hero, the middle of every road worth watching, and the foot and the middle of every landmark. 61 are seen from the opening view. The six that are not:

- the far side of Connaught Place's Outer Circle, behind the ring of blocks;
- the south half of Chandni Chowk, behind its own south side;
- the west face of the Lahori Gate, which looks away from the view;
- the foot of Firoz Shah's tomb, behind the madrasa's arm, and Deer Park behind the other arm;
- one point inside the dome it was asked about.

What the first try had wrong, and what was moved: the rally's line-up stood behind the stadium, and Khan Market's car park behind the market, so both forecourts are on the south-east side now. Bharat Mandapam's hall hid the foot of the Delhi Gate and stands 10 further south. Hauz Khas Village hid the tank.

## Liberties taken with the real place

So far, in the layout:

- **Hauz Khas is turned half round**, so that the tank lies on the side the tile is looked at from, and the madrasa is seen across the water, as from Deer Park. The village is behind it.
- **The Ring Road is not a ring.** The tile has its south side and its side by the river. It ends in a loop at Dhaula Kuan, and at the Signature Bridge it turns onto the bridge.
- **The Signature Bridge stands just north of the Red Fort.** In life it is eight kilometres upstream.
- **The road down the east bank ends at Akshardham's gate.**
- **The Pink Line is on a viaduct the whole way.** In life it goes underground east of Moti Bagh. Its station on the east bank stands for Mayur Vihar.
- **Humayun's Tomb and the National Stadium stand beside the Ring Road.** Mathura Road, which in life runs between them and India Gate, is left out.
- **Purana Qila is left out.** There was no ground east of the hexagon for it.
- **Left out for care**, as the research note's section 8 says: the Taj Mahal, the new Parliament and the Central Vista's new blocks, the mosque at the Qutub Minar's foot, Rajghat, Nizamuddin's shrine, and any parade.

## When `main` is merged

The main session is changing the engine while this tile is planned, and says when to merge. What that merge brings, and what the tile then owes:

- **The tile's place in the world.** The `TileDef` gets `place: { lon: 77.2, lat: 28.6 }`, for its pin in the map the table stands on.
- **`wimmel.fights('delhi')`** lists surfaces that lie in one plane. The tile must pass it.
- **The view tilts up and down.** The tile is looked at from low angles as well as from above, so whatever is seen from below needs its underside: the flyovers, the Metro's viaduct, the bridge's deck, every canopy.
- **Road markings are cleared wherever two streets overlap**, not only where they cross.
- **The screen for music** works from then on, and shows the tile's own picture when nothing is playing.
